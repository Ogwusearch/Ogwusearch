# Ogwusearch Engineering Notes

A structured engineering knowledge base for technical concepts,
calculations, standards, design decisions, testing, research,
field observations, and engineering software architecture.

## Purpose

Engineering-Notes is the documentation and reference layer of the
Ogwusearch Engineering ecosystem.

It records engineering knowledge in a reusable, traceable format.
Executable calculations remain in their respective engineering
packages.

## Structure

- `fundamentals/` — engineering principles and core concepts.
- `disciplines/` — discipline-specific knowledge.
- `calculations/` — calculation methods and worked examples.
- `standards/` — standards, codes, and compliance references.
- `design/` — design criteria and technical decisions.
- `materials-and-components/` — equipment and material references.
- `field-notes/` — site observations and practical findings.
- `testing-and-validation/` — verification methods and test records.
- `research/` — papers, datasheets, and research summaries.
- `assumptions-and-limitations/` — assumptions, risks, and limitations.
- `software-architecture/` — engineering software design and contracts.
- `case-studies/` — documented projects and lessons learned.
- `glossary/` — definitions and terminology.
- `templates/` — reusable note templates.
- `archive/` — superseded notes retained for traceability.

## Engineering Note Principles

1. Record units explicitly.
2. Distinguish verified facts from assumptions.
3. Show calculation methods and worked examples.
4. Record sources and their revisions where known.
5. Never silently change an established formula.
6. Document limitations and applicable operating conditions.
7. Keep software implementation separate from explanatory notes.
8. Review safety-critical information against authoritative sources.

## Suggested Workflow

1. Create a note using a template.
2. Assign a unique note ID.
3. Record inputs, units, methods, and assumptions.
4. Verify equations and examples.
5. Add authoritative references.
6. Review and update the note status.
7. Link related notes and engineering packages.

## Note Status

- `draft` — work in progress.
- `review` — awaiting technical verification.
- `verified` — reviewed against recorded sources and checks.
- `superseded` — replaced by a newer note.

A `verified` status does not itself establish regulatory compliance
or replace professional engineering review.
